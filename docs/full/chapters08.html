Enhancing Collaboration Between M&A and Development Teams

Successful project acquisition and development hinge on effective collaboration between commercial (M&A) teams and development teams. The consequences of misalignment can include inefficiencies, project delays, cost overruns and strained workplace relationships. Successful transaction teams establish clear ownership responsibilities, regular status reviews, and structured follow-up procedures to prevent unresolved defects from delaying financing or closing.

Team Alignment Is a Deal Accelerator

M&A alignment between commercial, development, legal, title, permitting, and financing teams can materially influence transaction outcomes. Misaligned assumptions regarding timelines, site control, permitting complexity, or construction sequencing often lead to valuation gaps, financing delays, cost overruns, and reduced project returns.

Renewable energy closings often depend on centralized transaction management. Unified closing protocols that coordinate legal documentation, title review, surveys, escrow instructions, lender requirements, and execution timelines can significantly reduce closing delays and improve financing certainty. Strategic communications, stakeholder engagement, and political awareness should also be integrated into broader project execution and risk management strategies.

Integrated Expert Teams

Transactions increasingly require early coordination among title professionals, legal counsel, environmental consultants, permitting specialists, engineers, surveyors, and financing teams. Siloed diligence processes can create conflicting assumptions, delayed issue identification, and avoidable transaction risk.

Many developers establish preferred relationships with surveyors, title professionals, environmental consultants, and legal teams familiar with renewable transactions. Repeat collaboration improves communication, accelerates issue resolution, and creates greater consistency across projects and portfolios. Integrated milestone calendars align permitting deadlines, title curative milestones, EPC schedules, PPA obligations, financing timelines, and COD targets into a centralized tracking framework shared across commercial and development teams.

Challenges in Team Collaboration

  • Different Objectives

Without clear direction commercial teams won’t necessarily confer with the development team because they have different directives. We’ve noticed that team collaboration is often problematic because they each have different objectives. The commercial teams are focused on deal closure, acquisition targets, and financial metrics. The development team is focused on project feasibility, technical viability and long-term operational success. This creates misalignment at the outset, which can lead to inefficiencies, increased costs and strained workplace relationships. can impact the culture. Team collaboration and transparency are essential for delivering the best service to our clients.

Communication Gaps

Compensation structures may encourage silos instead of teamwork. When individuals work independently, they may prefer to keep their knowledge private and might not demonstrate a high level of transparency or collaboration. Lack of information sharing can result in development teams being unprepared for inherited issues. When these conditions are present, there is an increased risk of project delays. Sometimes, after a transaction reaches the development team, they’ve had to revisit or renegotiate key project aspects because information wasn’t fully shared throughout the process.

Transparency & Collaboration

Lack of transparency or lack of collaboration can cost valuable time. This is particularly noteworthy under current market conditions, where we only have about 24 months to monetize key projects.

Transparency and collaboration are essential. Overlooking development concerns can lead to unexpected costs and frustration. When teams feel undervalued or overburdened, it can affect morale. Timing is critical, we must ensure teams have the necessary tools, structure, and environment they need to work efficiently and effectively.

Strategies for Enhancing Collaboration

Commercial and real estate teams working together should not be wishful thinking.

  • Early Involvement: Include development team representatives in M&A discussions from the outset. Ensure they have input on technical and operational considerations.
  • Regular Communication: Establish routine meetings between teams to discuss progress and challenges. Use shared platforms for documentation and updates.
  • Aligned Incentives: Create compensation structures that reward both deal closure and successful project implementation. Encourage team-based performance metrics.
  • Shared Goals: Define common objectives that encompass both acquisition and development success. Foster a culture of collaboration and mutual respect, create a safe space to ask questions and seek guidance.
  • Cross-Functional Training: Provide opportunities for team members to learn about each other’s roles and challenges. Promote understanding of how decisions in one area impact the other.

Collaboration & Communication Improve Outcomes

A collaborative environment can contribute to improved project outcomes. You will be more likely to meet critical deadlines and stay within projected budgets. Potential risks can be identified and mitigated earlier. This also creates culture for innovation, because diverse perspectives can lead to better problem solving.

We believe it’s important for project teams to understand the entire process. When we train teams, we always encourage as many people as possible to participate. This includes people who are ancillary, because it gives them the opportunity to learn about the many facets of the project. We have found that by understanding one another’s roles and challenges, they are more emotionally invested in the project. Connecting with one another as human beings, promotes enhanced collaboration and facilitates the cultivation of a skilled and dependable team, which is critical for achieving optimal operational performance.

Benefits of Effective Collaboration

  • Improved Project Outcomes: Projects are more likely to meet timelines and budget when teams work cohesively.
  • Risk Mitigation: Potential issues are identified and addressed early.
  • Enhanced Innovation: Diverse perspectives lead to better problem-solving.

Key Takeaway

Bridging the gap between commercial and development teams is essential for the seamless transition from acquisition to operation, ultimately contributing to the project’s success.
