Acknowledgments

Every successful project develops more than megawatts; it develops relationships, trust, and lessons learned. I have been fortunate to work alongside extraordinary professionals who have taught me that great projects are built not only on engineering and finance, but on integrity, curiosity, and collaboration. This book is dedicated to all of you who continue to raise the standard for our industry and who believe that doing things the right way is the only way.

As I reflect on the journey that led to this book, I realize that the most valuable lessons were never learned from documents or transactions alone. They came from people, those who asked difficult questions, challenged conventional thinking, shared their experiences, and remained committed to doing what was right, even when it wasn’t the easiest path. It comes from understanding the consequences when the right questions aren’t asked. Curiosity, thoroughness, integrity, collaboration, and disciplined risk management are at the heart of this book and the lessons learned.

The renewable energy industry is built on innovation, but its true foundation is trust. Every successful acquisition, every financed project, and every power plant that delivers electricity to our communities depends upon countless individuals working together with integrity, expertise, and a shared commitment to excellence. I have been extraordinarily fortunate to work beside many of those individuals throughout my career.

My deepest gratitude goes to the developers, investors, lenders, attorneys, title professionals, engineers, surveyors, environmental consultants, permitting specialists, landowners, and project managers who have shared their knowledge over the years. Every transaction, every challenge, and every solution contributed another lesson in understanding the complexities of utility-scale power development. Your willingness to share your successes as well as your hard-earned lessons made this book possible.

I would also like to thank the many industry professionals who generously contributed chapters and case studies, who challenged assumptions, offered practical insights, and strengthened the content. Your willingness to share your expertise has made this work more accurate, balanced, and valuable for future readers.

Special thanks are extended to:
