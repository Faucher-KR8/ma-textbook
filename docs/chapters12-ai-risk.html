Chapter 12: AI — The New Frontier in Risk Modeling & Scenario Analysis

Note

Editor’s Note: This chapter is currently being developed. Final content, case studies, and thought leader contributions will be provided by the author team. The framework and section headings below outline the intended chapter structure.

Overview

Artificial intelligence and machine learning are beginning to reshape how renewable energy developers, investors, and lenders assess, model, and mitigate transaction risk. From automated title document review to predictive permitting risk scoring and real-time portfolio monitoring, AI tools are creating new capabilities that compress timelines, surface hidden risks, and improve decision-making across the M&A lifecycle.

This chapter explores the frontier of AI-enabled risk modeling in renewable energy M&A — where the tools are today, where they are headed, and what practitioners need to understand to use them effectively without over-relying on them.
